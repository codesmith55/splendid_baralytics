## Build descriptions

**DexterStyx — Anti-Canyon (Ryan's seat).** Standard 3 mex 2 solar bot lab (lab done
0:41), com into wind, then a **Pawn raid** (≈10 Pawns by 2:24) with an early nano turret
(1:24) to keep the lab saturated. From 2:30 he expands hard — **8 more mex by 4:36** —
while shifting the factory to Rocketeers (~10) then a **13-Mace assault wave at 4:14–4:56**.
The economy climbs cleanly (no stalls; stored metal banks to 1600+ by 8:00) and the army
value peaks at **2528 at 8:00**, right when he **kills phorofor's commander (8:15)**.
Read: textbook aggressive AC — fast eco + relentless bot pressure that wins the canyon.

**phorofor — Canyon.** Same 3 mex 2 solar lab (0:44), but commits to a **10-Tick scout
flood** then a **huge Rocketeer mass (~20 by 3:53)** with LLT/Beamer to hold, and a late
**6-Lazarus reclaim** pack. The problem is energy: only 4 wind + 3 solar against a heavy
factory + 1530 BP, so he **stalls energy hard (stored E = 1 at 4:00, 0 at 6:00)** and
can't convert his build power into units fast enough. Army peaks 1440 at 4:00 then fades;
**commander dies 8:15** and his value collapses. Read: over-invested in army/BP without
the energy to sustain it — the Canyon hold broke.

**ElvisPresleySandwich — Back Canyon.** 3 mex 2 solar lab (0:45) into **Grunt + Thug
mass** (~15 Thugs by 4:02) with a little wind and one Guard LLT. But he **never expands
past 6 mex, never builds a converter, never reaches T2** — income flatlines at 13 m/s.
Army peaks 1106 at 4:00 and is **steadily ground down to 182 by 10:00**. Read: a one-note
T1 bot line on a thin economy; out-scaled and worn out in the lane.

  minute). None of the three built an energy converter in the live game, so **Conv = 0**
  and **MexM/s = total M/s** (minus negligible reclaim). `team_stats` and `unit_resources`
  were near-empty in this run, so a finer mex/reclaim split wasn't available.
- **Positions** were derived from commander start coordinates vs
  `all-that-glitters.json` (top-team slots via point-reflection); corroborated by the
  matchup outcome (AC beats Canyon).
