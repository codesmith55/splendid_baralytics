Metal + Energy = BP
Metal + No Energy = Solar
Default = Wind
Metal + Energy + BP = Estore (1/3 CT)
No Metal + Energy + Estorage = Reclaim Solars
No Metal + Energy + No Solars = EConverters
 
 Open a Skirmish lobby and pick a map(glitters fine), turn game mode ending = never, play the first 5 minutes and then pause. 
 Zoom out, holding shift select everything and look in the bottom left to see how much metal and energy everything you have is worth, add your current metal and energy. Back to lobby try again and compare or move on to the next build mode. 
 
 Build Order Modes: 
 Mode 1: Suggest opening 3 mex > 2 solar > factory > wind. Only Workers, focus on your eco and expansion without thinking about units. Prioritize using spacebar(insert) micro to utilize the eco rules. When you have metal and energy, tell your commander to make more workers to expand in more directions. Workers all just expand out. When you don't have energy, make more wind or solar. When you run out of metal, start reclaiming solars and making econverters.
